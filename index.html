Who we are

About us

Manblue Industries is an integrated mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) engineering company based in Krishnagiri district, Tamil Nadu. Under the leadership of Managing Director R T Raj Varshan, B.E, we were built on one conviction: industrial facilities perform best when a single, accountable team looks after the systems that keep them running.

Our origin. Our roots are in the industrial community of Krishnagiri, and our work grew from the practical needs of the plants around us: dependable installation, responsive repair and skilled fabrication, available from one team. Being local means we understand the sites we serve, and we are close enough to respond when it matters.

What we do

We install, commission, manufacture and maintain the critical systems of industrial, commercial and residential spaces, from machinery and overhead cranes to electrical networks, control panels and plumbing, delivered to your drawings and tested before handover.

  • 01

    Service

    Installation and commissioning of machinery, cranes, electrical and plumbing systems, delivered on time and tested before handover.

  • 02

    Manufacturing

  • 03

    Maintenance

    Planned and responsive upkeep, from motors and hydraulics to panels and piping, that limits downtime and keeps your plant running.
